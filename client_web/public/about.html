FolkIdle – a Slavic folklore idle RPG

A free game for the browser and Android, set in the world of Slavic legend. Raise a family line of heroes, send them to fight across five regions, gather materials and build a village. The game keeps playing after you close it.

What FolkIdle is

FolkIdle is an idle RPG (also called an incremental game). Your characters fight, chop, mine and fish on their own. You decide who does what, what they wear and what you invest in. When you close the page, the server works out what your characters would have done in the meantime. When you return, you get a summary of the loot and experience.

Five regions and their bosses

The world has five regions, each with four regular monsters and a boss. Beating a boss opens the next region, its gear and a new playable race.

  • Sunlit Plainsboss: Alpha Wolf
  • Whispering Woodsboss: Shadow Lynx
  • Scorched Wastelandboss: Magma Wyrm
  • Frozen Peaksboss: Frost Titan
  • Shadow Citadelboss: Malakor

Six races from Slavic legend

You start as a Human. Each boss you beat unlocks another race: the Fairy (vila), the Draugr, the Leshy, the Vodyanoy (vodník) and the Bies. Every race has its own aptitudes, and mastering a race adds more.

  • Human – more gold from kills, more experience and backpack space.
  • Fairy – dodge, attack speed and critical hits.
  • Draugr – more health and armour and lifesteal, at the cost of a slower swing.
  • Leshy – a bigger mining yield.
  • Vodyanoy – faster healing between fights, resistance to critical hits and longer offline progress.
  • Bies – a bigger yield from woodcutting, fishing and herbs.

Characters of any two races may marry, and the child is born as one of its parents' races. A strong line can carry its aptitudes into every race you have unlocked.

Family, ageing and rebirth

Characters age, marry and have children who inherit their parents' strengths. Up to three characters work at once, each at a different kind of work: one fighter, one woodcutter, one miner. A rebirth starts a life over, but the line keeps permanent bonuses.

Crafts, the village and gear

Four gathering crafts

Woodcutting, mining, fishing and herbalism, each with its own mastery. Fish stock the larder, which heals a character automatically in a fight. Herbs go into potions.

The village

Wood and ore build the village. The Town Hall earns gold, the Lumberjack and the Mine produce materials even while you are offline, and the Warehouse keeps them until you return. The Inn brings new villagers.

Gear

Eleven equipment slots including the axe, pickaxe and rod, fourteen rarity tiers, random affixes you can reroll, fusion, armour sets with set bonuses, and pets. Most of a character's power is gear, not level.

Together with other players

Guilds with roles and shared buffs, the world boss Chernobog that the whole server fights together, leaderboards, a market, chat and mail. Plus expeditions, dungeons, daily missions and a free seasonal Chronicle with fifty levels of rewards.

FAQ

Is FolkIdle free?
Yes. The game is free in the browser and on Android.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Any modern browser on a computer or phone works. There is also an Android app.
Does the game keep going when I am offline?
Yes. Your characters keep working, and you get a summary of what they earned when you return.
Do I need an account?
No. You can start as a guest. An account keeps your progress across devices.
